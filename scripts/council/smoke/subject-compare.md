# Compare: kettle rota A vs kettle rota B
Base: the office has one kettle and eleven people
Branch A: a rota pins each person to a two-minute window
Branch B: a second kettle sits beside the first one

## Side A — summary

Rota with named slots, no cost, needs daily upkeep.

## Side A — full outline

Everyone gets a slot. The rota lives on the wall. Slots slip when meetings
run over. One person keeps the rota current.

## Side B — summary

Second kettle beside the first, costs about forty pounds, no upkeep.

## Side B — full outline

Counter has space. Socket has two free outlets. Queue clears on its own.
Nobody maintains anything.
