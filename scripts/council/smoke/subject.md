# Proposal: a second kettle for the office kitchen

The office has one kettle and eleven people. At 09:00 a queue forms and stays
until about 09:20. Buying a second kettle would cost roughly forty pounds and
would sit on the counter beside the first one.

The counter has space. The socket beside the counter has two free outlets.
Nobody has asked for a second kettle, but three people have complained about
the queue.
